All stats

Andrea Kimi Antonelli, commonly known by his middle name, Kimi, was born on 25 August 2006 in Bologna, Emilia-Romagna, Italy. His father, Marco Antonelli, is a sportscar racing driver and owner of the San Marino–based AKM Motorsport racing team. By round 10 of the 2025 season, Antonelli secured his first F1 podium (P3) at the 2025 Canadian Grand Prix, to become the third youngest driver to step onto the rostrum, following on from his Sprint pole position in F1 at the 2025 Miami Grand Prix Sprint Race on May 3, 2025, the youngest polesitter of ‘any race type’ in F1 history.

In less than two seasons Kimi has acquired 8 race wins, matching his teammate George Russell. In 2025 he replaced 7-time world champion Lewis Hamilton, which instilled a lot of doubt in many fans. Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff was criticized heavily for this decision – replacing the most successful driver of F1 with a teenager?! Hamilton now races for Ferrari and has not been performing as well as Antonelli, so Mercedes team principal is content with his decision.

How can he win the Championship?

Currently Antonelli is leading the championship by 102 points. He could become the youngest world champion of Formula One even before his second season has finished – in Austin, Texas Grand Prix on October 25, 2026. Currently, Sebastian Vettel holds the youngest champion title; he was 23 years and 134 days old when he won in 2010 with Red Bull Racing. The permutations for Antonelli to become champion in Austin are:
Antonelli wins the race (25 points)
and
Russell finishes ninth or lower (2 points or less)
and
Hamilton finishes fifth or lower (10 points or less).
